The Garden Gate

You arrive at a closed garden gate. Opening it is the check-in ritual: a calm, ceremonial way into the garden, then the usual game-like home screen.

1Arriveclosed gate, first open of the day

Wooden double gate in a mossy stone arch with roses. Warm light leaks through the slats. One button; nothing else competes.

2The gate opens1.5 s transition

Doors swing wide, light floods in, petals burst toward you. Tapping anywhere skips it. After the first visit of the day only; later visits skip straight to home.

3Garden homethe main experience

Full garden with dock, atmosphere chip and a "nothing wilted" welcome. Objects (tree house, pond, mailbox, cottage) are all tappable.

4Check-inchoose any feelings, set intensity

Tap flowers to select several; petals set how strongly. Optional words come on the next card, never required.

5Seeds settlegarden reacts, no judgement

The sheet dissolves and new seeds pop into the meadow with soft rings. Sadness brings gentle rain, never wilting.

6Journal scrapbookthe month as a keepsake

Entries become taped notes and pressed flowers, with a calendar, timeline and garden history alongside.

The Floating Island

The whole garden is a small floating world. Camera moves are the navigation: drop in to tend it, pull back to travel between islands. Feels like a collectible diorama.

1Arrivethe sky matches the real hour

Your island hovers in a dawn sky, bobbing gently. Title above, one button below.

2Descend1.2 s camera dive

Clouds streak past as the camera drops onto the grass. Skippable by tap.

3Island hometend, wander, tap things

Island fills the screen. Hotspots (pond, tree house, mailbox) pulse faintly and show a label on hover. The dock and atmosphere chip are unchanged.

4Check-infeelings float up like seed packets

A drift of seed bubbles rises from below. Tap to catch several; each shows its flower and a petal intensity. Optional words come next.

5The island growsnew plants + a soft glow

Seeds drop onto the island with sparkles. Heavier feelings bring light rain; the island gets a little bigger as it fills.

6Moonlit islandnight, fireflies, glowing plants

After dark the sky deepens, lanterns and windows light up, and night-bloomers and hopeful seedlings glow.

7ArchipelagoExplore: pull back to visit other places

Pinch or tap Explore to pull the camera back. Each zone is its own island joined by rope bridges: the Meadow (home), the Pond, the Greenhouse, the Memory Tree, the Moon Garden, and a hazy Secret Corner that appears with time.
