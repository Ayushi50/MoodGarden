Landing page directions

Four ways to open Mood Garden. The garden stays the star in all of them.

1The Garden GateA ceremonial first impression · enter with one tap
Mood Garden
A garden that makes room for everything.

Warm, storybook, and calm. The ivy-and-stone arch frames the real garden behind it, petals drift past the gate, and the single button feels like opening a door. Best for first visit and as a quiet splash every time you return.

3The Floating IslandA little world you hold in your hands · diorama, not landscape
Mood Garden
your feelings, growing
Saturday · autumn · a few clouds

Most "game-like": the whole garden is one floating diorama that gently bobs. Tapping it zooms down into the full garden. Dawn/dusk skies change with the real time of day, and each new plant adds to the island.

4The Storybook OpeningA diary spread · personal and quiet, feels like a keepsake
Saturday, 21 November
Autumn · light rain in the garden
Welcome back.
Nothing wilted while you were away. The hosta grew a little, and something new is waiting by the mailbox.
What made you smile unexpectedly?

The most intimate: an open diary with a pressed flower, washi tape, a gentle prompt and a taped-in snapshot of the live garden. Tapping the photo "turns the page" into the full garden. Great tone, but one extra step before the garden.
